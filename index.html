FAQs

How can I find a room?
Use the search box or check the campus map to locate buildings, classrooms, and other facilities.

How do I search for a location?
Enter the name of the building, office, room, or facility in the search box and click “Find Location.”

Where can I find the Administration Building?
The Administration Building is located near the center of the campus. It includes offices such as the Registrar, SAS Coordinator, Scholarship, and other administrative offices.

Where can I find the HM Building?
Use the campus map and search for “HM Building” to locate it quickly.

Where can I find the Gym and Sports Court?
Both facilities are shown on the campus map and can be located using the search feature.

Where can I see announcements?
Visit the Announcements page through the Quick Links section for school updates and events.

What if my location cannot be found?
Check the spelling of your search and try using a shorter location name.

Who can use the Campus Navigator?
The Campus Navigator can be used by students, faculty, staff, and visitors.

Can I use the map on my phone?
Yes. The campus page is designed to adjust to smaller screens such as mobile phones and tablets.

What information can I find?
You can find campus locations, buildings, offices, facilities, class schedules, and other important campus information.

What should I do if I need assistance?
You may visit the appropriate office or ask the assigned staff for help with your concern.

Where can I ask for help if I have a school concern?
You can approach the appropriate school office or staff member for assistance.
